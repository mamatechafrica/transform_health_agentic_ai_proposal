Fee assumptions

The fee includes

  • Technical design
  • Agentic workflow development
  • WordPress-facing interface integration
  • Testing and refinement
  • Documentation
  • Handover

The fee excludes

  • Model/API usage
  • OCR services
  • Cloud hosting
  • Email delivery services
  • Premium WordPress plugins
  • Long-term maintenance after handover

These excluded costs would be identified and estimated during the orientation and technical design phase. Because the expected assessment volume is low, the proposed architecture would prioritise on-demand processing and configurable usage controls.

Note: USD 13,555 covers the deliverables; metered third-party operating costs are pass-through and will be estimated during design.
