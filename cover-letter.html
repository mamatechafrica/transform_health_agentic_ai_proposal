Dear Transform Health Team,

I previously delivered the Women Leaders Database for Transform Health, and I am pleased to submit my application for the consultancy to design, develop, test and integrate an agentic feature into the Health Data Governance Legislative and Regulatory Assessment Tool. For members of the review team who may not have been involved in that engagement, I have summarised my relevant experience and approach below.

I am a systems architect, product technology lead and digital health technology developer working across AI-enabled systems, structured data platforms and user-centred digital products. I would lead this assignment through Mama Tech, working with AI/ML Fullstack Developer Linah Muhonja to ensure that the system is technically reliable, evidence-grounded, cost-efficient and usable by non-technical users.

Beyond that track record with Transform Health, the most directly relevant outside project is Pactly, an AI-powered contract management system built by Mama Tech's AI/ML Fullstack Developer Linah Muhonja. It is designed to transform unstructured documents into structured, searchable and access-controlled information, covering document upload, PDF text extraction, structured metadata extraction, human-in-the-loop verification, semantic retrieval, workflow orchestration, background processing and tenant-aware access control.

A central design principle in Pactly is that AI-generated information is treated as a proposal rather than an authoritative finding. Extracted information must be reviewed and approved by a human before it becomes authoritative or is used for downstream retrieval. This principle is directly relevant to Transform Health's requirement for an evidence-extraction tool that must not invent, paraphrase or present unsupported interpretations of legislative provisions.

I also delivered, as Mama Tech, AskRafikey, an AI-powered SRHR chatbot designed to provide accessible health information to non-technical users. The platform is production-facing health technology delivered as a progressive web app (PWA) — no app store, no download, low-data accessible — with user accounts, responsive real-time messaging, conversation history, feedback tracking, offline support and notification functionality. Coalition staff update content themselves through an admin CMS without needing developers, and the system is GDPR-compliant with user data controls and anonymised analytics. It includes a bi-annual content audit workflow built into the admin system, runs on an OpenAI + Render + GitHub stack that is fully documented for handover, and launched with free support committed through 31 October 2025. Delivered at a project value of approximately USD 27,800 and documented in the AskRafikey case study: rafikey-portfolio.lovable.app.

Together, these projects demonstrate the experience the Mama Tech team brings across:

  • AI-assisted document and knowledge workflows
  • Structured data extraction
  • Human verification and controlled AI outputs
  • Semantic retrieval and document processing
  • Background and asynchronous processing
  • Access control and privacy-conscious architecture
  • Health-sector technology

My proposed approach treats the feature as an evidence extraction and traceability system, not a legal interpretation engine. Step 2 outputs would contain only structured mappings, verbatim extracts and source citations. Where relevant evidence is not found, the system would return "Not identified" rather than generate a speculative result. Step 3 would be generated using transparent, deterministic rules applied to the verified Step 2 output.

The proposed delivery would cover requirements and technical design, agentic workflow development, WordPress-facing interface integration, multilingual processing for English, French and Spanish, accuracy testing, user acceptance testing, deployment and handover.

I propose a total consultancy fee of USD 13,555, with a detailed breakdown included in the accompanying proposal.
